# Pulsenmore Ltd. investor relations landing page (concept)

An independent design concept for Pulsenmore Ltd. (Nasdaq and TASE: PLSM). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or prepared by the company, and it is not investment advice.

Every figure on the page is taken from the company's Form 20-F for fiscal 2025 and its Forms 6-K on SEC EDGAR, and is labelled filed, company figure, computed or as cited. The company reports in New Israeli Shekels under IFRS. Verify anything that matters against the filings.

The live page is served from `index.html` through GitHub Pages.
